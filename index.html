Why do some people drive you up the wall?

People behave in four main ways, and the author gives each a colour. Learn to spot the colour and a lot of "idiots" turn out to be people who work differently from you.

I'll guide you. Every lesson has the same four steps: why it matters, a short story, something to play with, and what to do with it tomorrow. No reading the book required.

Meet the four colours

Picture four friends planning a weekend trip. Each reacts in their own way.

Dominance (Red) takes charge. Inducement (Yellow) wins people over. Submission (Green) is steady and supportive; it doesn't mean weak. Analytic (Blue) checks and compares.
